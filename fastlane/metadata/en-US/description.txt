Sichtwort builds sight-word fluency: a word flashes briefly, the child recognizes it as a whole and says it aloud. Frequent words become automatic — freeing up attention for reading comprehension.

The app is German-first (basic vocabulary of the 500 and 1000 most frequent German words plus themed spelling lists) and also includes English Dolch and Fry sight words. The interface is available in German and English.

COMPLETELY FREE
No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions. Sichtwort is an open-source project by EÖDL (First Austrian Umbrella Association for Dyslexia).

NO DATA COLLECTION
No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Works fully offline.

FEATURES
• Flash time adjustable from 0.02 to 5 seconds
• Together mode (an adult listens and judges) and solo typing mode
• Built-in speech output for every word (on-device, offline)
• Syllable visualization: colored or appearing one by one
• Font choice: standard, OpenDyslexic or Lexend, adjustable size
• Adventure mode with adaptive difficulty
• Missed words return automatically until mastered
• Profiles for several children with individual statistics
• Custom word lists with CSV import AND export
• Reward system with collectible characters and endless levels
• iPhone and iPad, landscape and Split View support
